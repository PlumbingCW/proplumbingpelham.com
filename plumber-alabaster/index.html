When the kitchen sink and the tub back up together

One slow sink usually means a clog in that fixture's own trap or branch. Two or three fixtures gurgling or backing up at once points further down, toward the main drain line that leaves the house. That is the situation where plain plunging stops helping, and a drain cleaning visit with a cable and a camera becomes the sensible next step.

A quick test saves a wasted call. Run the washing machine drain or flush a toilet and see whether the tub bubbles or water rises in a floor drain. If it does, the blockage is shared, so stop all water use in the house until a plumber has opened the line.

What a camera shows that an address cannot

Alabaster began in the early 1920s as housing for the Alabaster Lime Company, became a city in 1953, and took in Siluria in 1971. A town put together in stages tells you nothing about the pipe under your own yard. Ask the plumber to put a camera in the line and show you the pipe on the screen before anyone proposes a repair.

Cold showers and a wet floor around the tank

Two different faults hide behind the words no hot water. A tank that is cold but dry is often a failed heating element, a tripped thermostat limit, or a pilot or gas control that has stopped working. A tank sitting in a puddle is a different job, because a steady leak from the tank body usually means the shell has failed.

For the second case, close the cold water valve above the heater. If the heater is electric and you can reach the breaker from dry footing, switch it off. If water is standing near the wiring or the panel, stay out and call from a dry spot. A water heater repair visit can fix a bad element, valve or thermostat, and the plumber will say plainly if a leaking tank is past repair.

Heater work falls under the plumbing codes the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board points to, which for homes means the 2021 International Residential Code. The board can tell you the current edition.

Limestone county, the Golly Hole and a dip in the yard
